## About

Strider Technologies is a strategic intelligence SaaS company. The platform
helps government agencies, defense contractors, universities, and private
sector organizations understand and mitigate nation-state threats — primarily
from China, Russia, North Korea, and Iran — targeting their workforce,
technology, supply chains, and IP.

Founded by former intelligence community professionals.
Headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah, USA.
